## Assets and content rules

- Do not use generative AI to create site content or assets. This covers images, video, audio, illustrations, icons and copy. Do not use AI upscalers or AI image/video tools on existing assets either.
- Only use royalty-free, copyright-free resources (for example CC0 / public domain, or stock libraries whose licence allows free commercial use without attribution, such as Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, Coverr and Unsplash).
- Before adding a third-party asset, confirm its licence on the source page and confirm it is not AI-generated (many stock sites label or host AI content; skip anything labelled or tagged as AI).
- Record every third-party asset in `ASSETS.md` with its source URL, author and licence.

## Development

When starting the dev server, use background mode:

```
astro dev --background
```

Manage the background server with `astro dev stop`, `astro dev status`, and `astro dev logs`.
